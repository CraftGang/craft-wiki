## 1. Install VectorCraft

The quickest way is [ArtCraft Launcher](https://github.com/storytold/craft-launcher/releases/latest): install it, then press **Install** on VectorCraft's card. To download VectorCraft on its own, see [Install and update](/apps/vectorcraft/install-update/).

## 2. Start a document

With no document open, VectorCraft shows its Home screen. Choose **New file**, or pick one of the sizes under **Quickly start a new file**, such as **Letter**, **A4**, **Web 1920×1080** or **Postcard**. **Open** opens a file you already have, and **Recent Files** lists the ones you used last.

**New file** opens the **New Document** dialog. Pick a category (**Mobile**, **Web**, **Print**, **Film & Video**, **Art & Illustration** or **Branding**) and a preset, change the size, units or number of artboards if you like, then choose **Create**.

## 3. Find your way around

- The **toolbar** down the left holds the tools. Press and hold a button with a small triangle to see the other tools in its group.
- The **Properties** panel on the right shows the settings for whatever is selected. **Layers** and **Libraries** sit beside it.
- The **contextual task bar** floats under the selection with the commands you're most likely to need next.
- The **status bar** at the bottom tells you what the current tool does with a click or a drag.
- Every panel is on the **Window** menu. To find any command by name, choose **Help › Search Commands**.

## 4. Draw and colour a shape

1. Choose the **Rectangle Tool** (**M**) or the **Ellipse Tool** (**L**), and drag on the artboard. Hold **Shift** to make a square or a circle.
2. Choose the **Selection Tool** (**V**) and click the shape to select it.
3. In the **Properties** panel, under **Appearance**, click the **Fill** swatch and pick a colour. Do the same for **Stroke**, and set its width beside it.

To draw your own shapes, use the **Pen Tool** (**P**): click to place corner points, and drag to make curves. See [Workflows](/apps/vectorcraft/workflows/#draw-and-edit-paths) for more.

**Ctrl+Z** (**⌘Z** on a Mac) undoes a step, and **Ctrl+Shift+Z** (**⌘⇧Z**) redoes it.

## 5. Save

Press **Ctrl+S** (**⌘S** on a Mac). A new document is saved as a `.vectorcraft` file, which keeps everything editable. To pick another format, such as SVG or PDF, choose **File › Save As**.

VectorCraft keeps recovery copies of unsaved work in the background. If it closes unexpectedly, it offers them back the next time it starts. See [Troubleshooting](/apps/vectorcraft/troubleshooting/#vectorcraft-closed-and-i-lost-my-work).

## 6. Export a picture

Choose **File › Export › Export As PNG** to save a picture of your artwork. For several sizes or formats at once, or one file per artboard, use **File › Export › Export for Screens**.
