## 1. Install ArtCraft Studio

Download it from the [ArtCraft website](https://getartcraft.com/) and install it. For the files on GitHub, see [Install and update](/apps/artcraft-studio/install-update/).

## 2. Sign in

ArtCraft Studio asks you to log in to your ArtCraft account when it starts.

- **Login with Website** opens the ArtCraft website in your browser. You can use Google there, or any account you're already signed in with on the website.
- **Scan to Login** shows a QR code to scan with your phone.

If you don't have an account yet, choose **Don't have an account?** to make one.

## 3. Find your way around

ArtCraft Studio opens on **Home**: "What will you craft today?". It lists every tool, under **Create** and **Edit**. The tabs at the top switch between **Home**, **Create Image**, **Create Video**, **Create Audio**, **Image Editor** and **3D Stage**.

On the right of the top bar are **My Library**, the task queue, **Settings** (the gear) and your credit balance.

## 4. Make an image

1. Choose **Create Image**.
2. Type what you want in the prompt box: "Describe what you want in the image...".
3. Choose a model. The picker also sets the **Aspect Ratio**, **Resolution** and the number of images.
4. Click the round arrow button to generate. Point at it first to see how many credits it costs.

The task queue in the top bar shows what's still generating. Finished images are saved to **My Library**.

## 5. Download it

1. Open **My Library**.
2. Click the image to view it.
3. Click **Download**.

The file goes to your **Downloads** folder. To choose another folder, see **Settings › Downloads**.

From the same view you can also **Edit Image**, **Make Video**, **Remove BG** or **Make 3D**.
