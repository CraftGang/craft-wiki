While you work, WordCraft keeps a copy of every document with unsaved changes, new drafts included. If WordCraft or your computer crashes, you can get them back.

## Get your work back

Open WordCraft again. If it has copies from the last time, it opens the **Recover** page and lists them. You can also open it at any time with **File › Recover**.

- **Restore** opens the copy as a new, unsaved document. Choose **File › Save As** to keep it. Restoring never changes your original file.
- **Discard** deletes the copy, after asking you first.
- **Continue without restoring** leaves the copies there for later.

If you had several documents open, each one has its own copy.

## What is kept

- A copy is made at most every 10 seconds, and only when the document has changed. The last few seconds of typing before a crash can be lost.
- When you save a document, or close it and choose not to save, its copy is deleted.
- Copies are kept until you save or discard them. They aren't deleted after a set time.
- Copies are kept only on your computer. There's no cloud backup or version history.

## Documents with a password

WordCraft doesn't keep recovery copies of password-protected documents, because the copy wouldn't be protected. Save these often.

## In the web version

The web version doesn't keep recovery copies. Save often.
