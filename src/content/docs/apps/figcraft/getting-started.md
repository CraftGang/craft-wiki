## 1. Install FigCraft

The quickest way is [ArtCraft Launcher](https://github.com/storytold/craft-launcher/releases/latest): install it, then press **Install** on FigCraft's card. To download FigCraft on its own, see [Install and update](/apps/figcraft/install-update/).

## 2. Open FigCraft

The first time, FigCraft opens on its **Home** screen. From here you can start a **New design file** or a **New board**, open a file, join a shared file with a code, or start from a template. After that, it opens straight into a new file. The house button at the top left of the window takes you back to Home at any time.

The **Templates** section has eight starter files: a mobile app, a landing page, a dashboard, a design system starter, a wireframe kit, social post sizes, presentation slides and an icon set starter. To look around before you design anything, open the **Mobile app** template: it has four screens built from components, with a prototype flow.

## 3. Find your way around

![The FigCraft window: the Layers panel on the left, three phone screens on the canvas, the toolbar at the bottom and the Design panel on the right with the Share button at its top](/assets/uploads/figcraft/hero.webp)

- **Left**: the **File** panel lists the file's pages and layers. **Assets** has your components, and **Variables** your variables. The button above them opens the main menu.
- **Bottom**: the toolbar, with the drawing tools on the left and a switcher for **Draw**, **Design**, **Prototype** and **Inspect** (Dev Mode) on the right.
- **Right**: the **Design** and **Prototype** panels for whatever you've selected, with **Share** and the play button (present) at the top.
- **Bottom right**: the **?** button has **Getting started** tips, **Keyboard shortcuts** and **Check for updates…**.

To find any command by name, press **Ctrl+K** (**⌘K** on a Mac) to open **Actions**.

## 4. Draw a frame

1. Press **F** for the **Frame** tool, then drag on the canvas. Or pick a device size from the Design panel.
2. Press **R** for a rectangle, **T** for text, or **O** for an ellipse, and draw inside the frame.
3. Press **V** to go back to the **Move** tool, and select a layer to change its fill, stroke and effects in the Design panel.

## 5. Add auto layout

Select a frame and press **Shift+A** (**Add auto layout** in the **Object** menu). Its children now line up in a row or column, with padding and gaps you set in the Design panel. Change a child's size and the frame grows or shrinks to fit.

## 6. Save

Press **Ctrl+S** (**⌘S** on a Mac). FigCraft saves a **.figcraft** file. To pick another name or save a copy as .fig, choose **File › Save as…** from the main menu.

FigCraft keeps a recovery copy of unsaved work in the background. If it closes unexpectedly, the next start opens the copy again for you to save. To save every change by itself, turn on **Save files automatically** in **Preferences**.

## 7. Export

Select a layer, then choose **File › Export…** (**Ctrl+Shift+E**, **⇧⌘E** on a Mac). Pick PNG at 1×, 2× or 3×, JPEG, WebP, SVG or PDF.
